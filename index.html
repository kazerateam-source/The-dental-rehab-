FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments are recommended so you are seen without waiting, but you can also call the clinic to ask about walk-ins.

Is treatment painful?

We aim to keep every treatment as comfortable as possible and will explain each step first. Tell us if you feel anxious.

How often should I get a check-up?

Most people benefit from a check-up and cleaning every six months, or as your dentist advises.

Do you treat children?

Yes, please ask the clinic about child-friendly dental care when you book.
